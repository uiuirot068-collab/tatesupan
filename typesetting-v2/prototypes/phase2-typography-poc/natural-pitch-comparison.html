C1-JUSTIFIED: Production's current per-preset gridMode (solid for A5 1段, justified-stretch elsewhere), corrected to real PX_PER_MM=2.2 screen scale for every preset including Web (fixing the P2-L03-diagnosed unit bug).

Line pitch (column-to-column spacing) is identical across all three for a given preset — only character (down-column) pitch differs. No punctuation/optical correction or preset-specific tuning was added anywhere below.
