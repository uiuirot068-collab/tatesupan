## Overall architectural finding

**No feature in this loop required abandoning determinism, source mapping, or keep-together guarantees to represent.** Every "NO" in the "rule frozen?" column is a **standards-evidence gap** (kinsoku table completeness, dash/ellipsis citation, long-ruby overflow spacing, TCY threshold) — not a case where the C1-NATURAL architecture had to fall back to browser-native flow to make something work. Per the loop's accept/reject rule, this is grounds for **PASS**, not HOLD: nothing here shows the architecture "cannot represent or control the required behavior without collapsing into renderer-specific hacks."
